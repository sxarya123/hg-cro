PROTECTION, WITHOUT THE CARD GRID

COVERAGE. EVIDENCE. RESOLUTION.

The redesign focuses the page on the moments that matter: whether a case is eligible, the 48-hour reporting window, evidence required, how investigation works, and the remedy if a valid recall claim is confirmed.

Quick summary

Eligible purchases receive account recall warranty protection. If you experience a possible recall, contact support within 48 hours, provide proof of purchase and screenshots, and stay responsive during the investigation. If a valid claim is confirmed, the standard remedy is store credit equal to the original purchase amount.

I.

Account Recall Warranty

When you purchase an account from Heaven Guardian, you are protected under our account recall warranty.

This warranty applies to eligible cases where an account is recalled, recovered, or taken back after purchase, subject to compliance with our Terms and security instructions.

Covered remedy

If a valid recall claim is confirmed, you will receive store credit equal to the original purchase price. This store credit can be used toward eligible services offered by Heaven Guardian.

II.

What Is Not Covered

The recall warranty is designed to protect against eligible account recall issues. It does not cover every type of game-related problem.

  • in-game bans or suspensions
  • bugs or technical issues inside the game
  • account locks caused by publisher security systems
  • rollbacks or changes made by the game developer
  • issues outside the scope of the stated warranty terms
Buyer security steps matter

Coverage may also be limited if required buyer security steps were not completed during or after the transaction process.

III.

If you notice unusual account activity, lost access, or signs of a recall, contact Heaven Guardian within 48 hours.

IV.

Investigation and Resolution Process

Once a case is submitted, the team will review the information and investigate the issue.

  • Most cases are reviewed as quickly as possible, and some may be resolved within hours.
  • More complex cases may take longer if additional evidence or verification is required.
  • If the account can be recovered, Heaven Guardian will assist in restoring access where possible.
  • If the issue qualifies under the warranty and recovery is not possible, the appropriate next step will be determined under the policy.
Third-party delays

Delays may occur when a case depends on third-party systems, game publisher actions, or support responses outside Heaven Guardian’s control.

V.

Case Closure Rules

To keep support cases active and fair, buyers must remain responsive during the review process.

Inactivity rule

If the buyer does not reply to messages, the case may be closed 1 week after the buyer’s last message.

Once a case is closed due to inactivity, the closure may be treated as final and irreversible. For the best chance of a fast resolution, respond promptly whenever the team requests more information or confirmation.
