Quick summary

Heaven Guardian may collect contact, transaction, communication, and technical information to operate services, support transactions, prevent fraud, improve the website, and comply with legal obligations. Cookies and analytics may be used where applicable, and users may request access, correction, or deletion subject to applicable law.

I.

Controller Information

Heaven Guardian is the controller of personal data collected through this website, unless otherwise stated.

Business NameHeaven Guardian
Emailcontact@heaven-guardian.com
Websitehttps://heaven-guardian.com/
II.

Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information collected when you visit our website, create or place an order, contact us, communicate with us through Discord or other support channels, or otherwise interact with Heaven Guardian services.

It does not automatically apply to third-party websites, platforms, payment providers, game publishers, or services that may be linked from our website or used during a transaction.

III.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact information such as your name, email address, Discord username, and other information you provide when contacting us.
  • Order and transaction information such as account details relevant to a purchase, payment status, billing-related information, and customer support records.
  • Communications such as messages, inquiries, complaints, feedback, and dispute information.
  • Technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, access time, referrer URL, and pages visited.
  • Marketing preferences if you subscribe to updates, newsletters, or promotional messages.
IV.

How We Collect Information

We may collect information directly from you when you:

  • contact us through email, forms, chat, or Discord
  • place an order or request one of our services
  • submit feedback, a complaint, or a dispute
  • subscribe to updates or promotions
  • browse our website and interact with cookies or analytics tools
Direct and automatic collection

Some information is provided by you, while other technical data may be collected automatically when you use the website.

V.

How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • provide, manage, and complete orders or services
  • verify transactions and reduce fraud or abuse
  • communicate with you about purchases, support requests, disputes, and account-related matters
  • improve our website, customer support, and service quality
  • maintain website security and investigate suspicious or unlawful activity
  • send service-related messages and, where permitted, marketing communications
  • comply with legal obligations and protect our legal interests
VI.

Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable under data protection law, we process personal data based on one or more of the following legal bases:

ContractTo provide requested services or manage transactions.
Legitimate interestsTo operate, secure, and improve our business and website.
ConsentWhere required, such as for certain cookies or optional marketing communications.
Legal obligationWhere processing is necessary to comply with applicable law.
VII.

Disclosure of Information

We may share personal information only where necessary for legitimate business or legal purposes, including with payment processors and billing providers, hosting and analytics providers, support or transaction-management tools, and legal or regulatory authorities where required.

We do not sell your personal information as part of our normal business operations.

If Heaven Guardian is involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law.

VIII.

Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to make the site function properly, understand traffic, improve performance, and support marketing activities.

01 / Essential

Essential Cookies

Necessary for core website functions such as security, session handling, and page functionality.

02 / Analytics

Analytics Cookies

Tools such as Google Analytics may help us understand pages visited, session duration, device information, browser details, and IP-related data.

03 / Advertising

Advertising and Remarketing

Advertising or remarketing tools may be used to show more relevant ads to previous visitors.

Your control

You can control or disable cookies through browser settings. Where required by law, non-essential cookies are used only after consent.

IX.

Server Logs and Technical Data

Like many websites, we may automatically collect general technical information in server logs, including browser type, operating system, referring pages, pages visited, timestamps, IP address, internet service provider, and diagnostic data.

We use this information to maintain security, diagnose issues, prevent abuse, and improve website performance.

X.

Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing services, handling disputes, meeting legal obligations, enforcing agreements, and preventing fraud.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

XI.

International Transfers

Some service providers may process data in countries outside your own. Where required by applicable law, we take reasonable steps to ensure that personal data transferred internationally receives an appropriate level of protection.

XII.

Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed to children who are not legally permitted to use them. Where a minor uses our services, it should only be with authorization and supervision from a parent or legal guardian where required by law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information without proper authorization, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.
